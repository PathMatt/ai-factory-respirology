Social history

67-year-old retired high-school chemistry teacher, 31 years in the classroom, retired 2019. Works part-time in a woodworking shop in the detached garage, mostly maple and oak; wears a dust mask inconsistently. Lives with spouse in a 1920s farmhouse on a rural property, on well water. The basement flooded in spring 2023; there is ongoing water staining and a musty smell in the back room. Heats with a forced-air gas furnace and a wood stove in winter.

Former smoker, 15 pack-years, quit 1998; no vaping or cannabis. Two to three glasses of red wine a week. Winters in Arizona each February for the past four years; Portugal in 2024; no known TB contacts.

Sleeps with a down duvet and two feather pillows, replaced about five years ago. Uses an outdoor hot tub on the back deck three to four evenings a week, year-round. Pets: an elderly Labrador; two budgerigars and a cockatiel, kept in the living room for 12 years, cage cleaned weekly by the patient. Grows vegetables and keeps a compost pile; no farm animals since childhood. Volunteers Saturdays at the local library.
